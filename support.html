Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored? Is it synced anywhere?

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, inside the app's own storage. There is no account, no server, and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes the data.

How do I move my data to a new device?

Use Export to save your data as a file, then use Import on the new device. On iPhone and iPad, the file is saved through the system Files app or the share sheet.

The charts look empty. What should I do?

Charts are generated from your asset units. Add at least one asset unit with an amount first, then open the relevant tab — asset structure, share analysis, trends, and rebalancing are all derived from the same data set.

How do I erase everything?

Use the Clear function inside the app, or simply delete the app from your device. Because nothing was ever uploaded, there is nothing to remove on a server.

Does the app collect any data or show ads?

No. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests are made through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to issue refunds directly.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad. Your data stays on the device you are using, and there is no sync between devices — use Export and Import if you want to keep more than one device up to date.

常见问题

数据存在哪里？会自动同步吗？

你录入的全部内容都只保存在本机 App 的存储空间里。没有账号、没有服务器、没有云同步。 删除 App 即同时删除数据。

怎么把数据迁到新设备？

先使用「导出」把数据存成文件，再在新设备上使用「导入」。 iPhone / iPad 上会通过系统「文件」或分享面板保存。

图表是空的，怎么办？

图表由你的资产单元数据生成。请先添加至少一个带金额的资产单元， 之后再看资产结构、占比分析、趋势、再平衡这几个页签，它们共用同一份数据。

怎么彻底清空数据？

在应用内使用「清空」功能，或直接卸载 App。由于数据从未上传，服务端没有需要删除的内容。

会收集数据或显示广告吗？

不会。应用内没有任何统计、广告、第三方 SDK 或追踪代码。 详见隐私政策。

怎么申请退款？

所有交易由 Apple 处理，退款请通过 Apple 提交： reportaproblem.apple.com。 我们无法直接为你办理退款。

支持哪些设备？

iPhone 与 iPad。数据保存在你当前使用的设备上，设备之间不会自动同步； 想让多台设备保持一致，请使用「导出 / 导入」。
